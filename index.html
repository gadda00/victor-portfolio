How It Works

Diagnose. Prove. Ship.

Pilot-to-production is where most AI dies. This engagement model is designed around getting past it.

1

Diagnose

Define the user, the constraint, and the success metric before any code is written — most "AI projects" fail here, not in the code.

2

Prove

Build the smallest useful slice and test it against real scenarios with a real evaluation set. Numbers or it didn't happen.

3

Ship

Harden, document, observe, and hand over a system your team can run — with dashboards to prove it stays healthy.

01
Fixed scope, fixed price.What we signed is what you pay. No surprise invoices, ever.
02
You own everything.Code, prompts, data, infrastructure accounts — transferred at handover.
03
Milestone delivery.Big builds run 30/40/30 — you see working software at every gate.
04
No lock-in.Leave anytime — with a running, documented system. Care is a service, not a hostage.
About

Hi, I'm Victor.

I'm an AI engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I started in data science for fintech and financial services — forecasting, fraud detection, customer segmentation — and moved to production AI full-time when generative models matured: multi-agent platforms, RAG pipelines, on-device ML, and multilingual assistants.

What I actually do is take technically difficult AI ideas and make them work under real constraints — $80 phones, Swahili and French delivery, regulated finance, weak connectivity. The systems below — and the client products above — are the evidence. I also write the engineering down, because work you can't explain is work you can't trust.

I take on select client work — audits, sprints, and full builds. If you have a problem that needs real engineering, let's talk.
