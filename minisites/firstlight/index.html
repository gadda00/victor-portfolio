Res-8 hexagonal zoning with per-cell need scoring — transparent prioritization, not a black box.

03

Dijkstra routing + isochrones

Reachability, not just distance — where can help actually get, and how fast.

04

Hash-verified sitreps

Situation reports sealed with FNV-1a hashes; tampering is detectable.

Provenance labeling

Simulated layers are labeled simulated — the demo never lies about its data.
