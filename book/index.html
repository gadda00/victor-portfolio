Who it's for

  • Founders and product/ops leaders with a valuable workflow that's slow, manual, or error-prone.
  • Teams with an AI feature that has quality, latency, cost, or trust problems.

What to bring (optional)

  • The workflow you care about and roughly how many users, transactions, or documents it touches.
  • Tools it lives in today (CRM, ERP, email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets).
  • Any AI you already tried — including the ones that disappointed you.

What you leave with

  • A build / no-build view on the workflow you brought.
