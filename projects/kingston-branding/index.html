01 What this system is

A print shop’s product is customization — your logo on a hoodie, your brand colours on a banner, five hundred business cards by Friday. Counter-only ordering caps that at walk-in traffic. This platform puts the whole counter online: a live product customizer, cart and guest checkout with M-Pesa, pickup or delivery, shareable order tracking, and an admin dashboard for the staff on the other side.

02 What I built

Live product customizer. Customers pick colours, sizes and materials, upload their logo or photo, add custom text — and watch the preview update as they configure. No design round-trips, no “what did you mean?” phone calls.

M-Pesa checkout. STK Push or Till Number payment — the two rails Nairobi customers actually use — with guest checkout (no account required to order) and a pickup-or-delivery choice.

Shareable order tracking. Every order gets a link with a five-stage status tracker — Pending → Confirmed → In Production → Ready → Delivered — so “is my order ready?” answers itself.

Admin dashboard. Staff manage orders, products and settings, and read contact messages — with the storefront catalog managed in Prisma.

03 Engineering notes

Dual-storage on Netlify, zero database setup. The read-only product catalog ships with the build (Prisma + bundled SQLite), while customer orders and contact messages persist to Netlify Blobs — a managed key-value store. The admin dashboard merges both sources automatically, so the whole store runs on Netlify without provisioning or paying for a database server. For full write capability (editing products from the dashboard), the repo documents a clean switch to Postgres.

04 Where it stands

The site was delivered and deployed for the client. The live URL recorded in the repository README (kingstonbranding.netlify.app) returned 404 when checked on 11 Nov 2026 — the deploy appears to be offline or renamed. The full source remains public on GitHub, and every claim on this page comes from that repository. I have not published the demo admin credentials anywhere on this site.
