01 What this system is

The suite started as the forecasting core of real engagements — retirement-benefits projections and fintech planning — where 'the model felt right' was never an acceptable answer. Every family (Holt-Winters, ARIMA/SARIMA, Prophet-style decomposition) is implemented, validated and backtested, with ensemble selection picking the honest winner per series.

It now powers Busara AI's forecasting stage: the same engines, promoted from consulting artifact to production dependency.
