01 What this system is

An awards platform does one job: make excellence visible. For ACLA's climate-leadership awards, that meant a platform that presents nominees and their work with the weight they deserve — interactive 3D moments via Three.js, a fast Next.js shell, and AI woven into the experience layer.

This engagement sits alongside Tapi Learn in the ACLA relationship: the same organization, a different surface — one powering learning, one powering recognition.
